Charter questions

Frequently asked questions

How much does bus hire cost on the Gold Coast in 2026?

Our Sprinter mini buses charter from $640 for a half day and $1,160 for a full day; the 53-seat luxury coach from $1,550 half day and $2,300 full day. One-way airport group transfers start at $150 (Sprinter 10) and $700 (coach). Every quote is all-inclusive — driver, fuel, tolls and GST.

Is the price per person or per vehicle?

Per vehicle — one fixed price covers the whole group. A full 15-seat Sprinter often works out cheaper per person than rideshares, with everyone travelling together.

Do you use subcontractors or brokers?

No. Every charter runs on a vehicle Sky Transfers owns, driven by our own professional chauffeurs. The vehicle in your quote is the vehicle that arrives.

Which bus size should I book for a wedding?

For up to 15 guests per trip, the Sprinter 15-Seater is our most-booked wedding vehicle; for 40+ guests moving at once, the 53-seat coach shuttles everyone in one run. Tell us your guest count and venues and we'll recommend the cheapest combination.

Do you run Gold Coast to Brisbane and Byron Bay charters?

Yes — Brisbane Airport, the Brisbane International Cruise Terminal, Byron Bay and Tamborine Mountain are all regular runs. Cross-border and hinterland itineraries are quoted individually, usually within the hour.

Can we bring drinks on board?

Water and soft drinks, absolutely. For alcohol, Queensland rules apply and it depends on the vehicle and occasion — ask when booking and we'll confirm what's possible for your charter.

How far ahead should we book a charter?

Weddings and school-holiday dates are best locked in 2–4 weeks ahead; corporate transfers and airport groups often just need a few days. Quotes are free and hold no obligation — call +61 481 437 772 for urgent dates.
