Bring bookings across from Limo Anywhere

Switch-over: Monday 26 October 2026. From then, new bookings go only into this system. Until then you can import as often as you like - a booking already brought across is never added twice.

  1. Open the BOOKINGS 2026 Google Sheet and click the LA Bookings tab.
  2. File → Download → Comma-separated values (.csv). That downloads just that tab.
  3. Choose the file below. Nothing is saved until you press Import.

Autopilot

The routine office work, done on a timer, with an email to you each time. It never messages a driver or a customer by itself: drivers still get jobs when you tap Send, and invoices go out only when you press Approve. Each part is off until you switch it on. It checks every five minutes.

Dispatch

The evening before, it picks a driver and car for every job that has none - free at that time, papers in date, the right class of car, staff before subcontractors, the lightest day first - and emails you one list ready to send. A booking made later, or a job a driver turns down, is picked again within minutes and you're told. A job you've touched yourself is left alone.

Closing jobs

A job still open some hours after it should have finished is chased by email. If the driver had reached the passenger (on location or passenger on board) and just forgot Done, it can be marked done for you.

Invoices

When an account's week, fortnight or month ends (set below), the invoice is issued and you get an email with an Approve button. Nothing goes to the customer until you press it.

Morning digest

One email each morning: today's jobs, and everything that needs a person - no driver, not sent, not accepted, not closed, invoices waiting or overdue, licences and regos about to expire.

Booking emails

Reads the bookings inbox (read-only - nothing is moved, deleted or marked read) for emails from the addresses on each account (Invoices tab → edit the account → Bookings come from). Each booking it can read goes on the board marked Check; anything unclear - a change, a cancellation, two bookings in one email, a missing time - is emailed to you to enter by hand. Only mail that arrives after you switch this on is read.

Google Calendar

Reads the bookings calendar (read-only - nothing is ever changed in it) every 5 minutes and puts each upcoming job on the board. Jobs it can read cleanly go straight on; anything unclear - no price, no phone, two vehicles, a time still TBC - is marked Check. A booking already here (a website or agent booking, or a Limo Anywhere import) is linked, not doubled. When an event changes, the job is not changed: it's marked Calendar changed and you apply it or keep the job. A deleted event marks its job Removed from calendar; cancelling it is up to you.

Guest updates

Messages to the guest: driver assigned the evening before (only once you've sent the job to the driver), on the way when the driver taps it, and arrived with where to meet. Each has a "track your driver" link. They show the driver's first name and the car - never the fare, the driver's number or a rego.
