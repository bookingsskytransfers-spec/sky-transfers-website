Fly into Gold Coast, not Brisbane

This is the single most useful thing on this page. Gold Coast Airport is roughly 67 km from Byron Bay — about an hour down the Pacific Motorway. Brisbane Airport is more than twice as far, and the transfer costs more than twice as much: $450 against $220 in a sedan.

If your flights are not locked in yet, checking Gold Coast Airport first is frequently worth a couple of hundred dollars on the ground leg alone. If they are already booked into Brisbane, the run is still a published fare rather than something you have to negotiate.

Crossing the border

Byron Bay is in New South Wales and both airports are in Queensland, so every one of these transfers crosses a state line. In practice that changes nothing for you — there is no border fee, no vehicle change, and it is already in the fare. It is worth knowing only because it is the reason some operators quote this run as a special case and price it accordingly.

One thing it does affect: New South Wales and Queensland keep different clocks in summer. From early October to early April, NSW observes daylight saving and Queensland does not, so Byron Bay runs an hour ahead of the Gold Coast. We schedule against your flight time in the airport's own timezone, which is the safe way round — but if you are giving us a pick-up time from a Byron address in summer, say which clock you mean.

Surfboards, and the rest of it

It is Byron. Boards, bikes and oversize bags are normal here rather than an exception. They travel free where they fit — and a longboard does not fit in a sedan. Book an SUV or a minivan, or add the $30 luggage trailer, and tell us what is coming so the right vehicle turns up rather than the right-sized car with a problem on the roof.

Questions

Is Bangalow more expensive than Byron Bay?

No. Bangalow, Suffolk Park, Broken Head and Ewingsdale are all priced identically to Byron Bay from either airport.

How long does it take from Brisbane Airport?

Around two and a half hours in normal conditions — it is roughly 180 km. Build in more on a Friday afternoon. From Gold Coast Airport it is about an hour.

Do you run late at night after a delayed flight?

Yes, 24 hours a day, at the same fare. There is no night rate on this route or any other, and flight tracking means a delay moves the pick-up without you having to call.

Can you collect from Byron for a morning flight?

Yes, and it is the same fare in reverse. For an early departure out of Gold Coast Airport we will recommend a pick-up time built back from your check-in cut-off — and in summer we will confirm which timezone we are both working in.

Is there a cheaper shared option?

Not from us — everything we run is a private vehicle at a per-vehicle price. For one or two people a shared shuttle will be cheaper. From about three people, with luggage, a private car is usually both faster and comparable.
