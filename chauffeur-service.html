What "chauffeur" means here

It is a word a lot of companies use loosely, so it is worth being plain about what it buys you with us. Every car is owned by Sky Transfers and every chauffeur is employed by Sky Transfers. Nothing is handed to a subcontractor on the morning, which is why the vehicle in your confirmation is the vehicle that turns up.

The practical difference from a taxi or a rideshare is that the trip is arranged before the day rather than during it. Your chauffeur has the flight number, the address and the route in advance. You get their name and mobile number before pick-up. On arrivals they wait inside the terminal holding a name board, not in a car park sending texts.

We are not a stretch-limousine company. If you want a stretch limo or a stretch Hummer for a formal or a party, we do not run them and we would rather tell you now than take the booking. What we have is a fleet of sedans, luxury sedans, people movers, minivans, Mercedes Sprinters and a 53-seat coach — see the fleet.

Two ways to book a chauffeur

Fixed fare, point to point

The usual choice for an airport run, a hotel transfer or a one-way trip between cities. The fare is set by suburb and vehicle and published before you book — it does not move with demand, the hour, or the day of the week. Brisbane to the Gold Coast, Brisbane Airport to the CBD, Coolangatta to Surfers Paradise: all priced on the price list, all tolls and GST included.

By the hour, as-directed

The chauffeur stays with you instead of dropping you and leaving. This is what suits a day of meetings across the city, a site inspection, a run of venues, or a visiting executive with a schedule that will change. Corporate and as-directed hire starts at $85 per hour; weddings and events start at $120 per hour, where the day needs planning rather than just driving.

Tell us the hours and the rough plan and we quote the block. It is usually cheaper than stringing together separate trips, and it removes the argument about who pays for waiting time.

Questions

What is the difference between a chauffeur and a taxi or rideshare?

A chauffeur is booked in advance and is assigned to you. The car is cleaned for your trip, the driver knows the route and the flight number before the day, and the price is agreed before you travel. A taxi or rideshare is whoever is nearest when you open the app, at whatever the meter or the algorithm says at that moment.

How much does a chauffeur cost in Brisbane or on the Gold Coast?

Two ways. Point to point is a fixed fare set by suburb and vehicle, from $80 in a sedan, published before you book. By the hour is from $85 for corporate and as-directed work, and from $120 for weddings and events, where the car and chauffeur stay with you for a block of time.

Can I book a chauffeur by the hour?

Yes. As-directed hire means the chauffeur stays with you rather than dropping you and leaving, which suits a day of meetings, a site inspection, or a run of venues. Tell us the hours and the rough plan and we quote a block rather than a series of separate trips.

Do you drive between Brisbane and the Gold Coast?

Constantly. Brisbane to the Gold Coast is one of the runs we do most, at a fixed fare that already includes the Gateway toll. It is a published price on every suburb page rather than something we quote on request.

Do you offer stretch limousines?

No. We run sedans, SUVs, people movers, luxury sedans and minivans, Mercedes Sprinters and a 53-seat coach. If you want a stretch limousine or a stretch Hummer for a formal or a party, we are not the right company and we would rather say so than take the booking.

Will I have the same chauffeur all day?

On an hourly booking, yes. The same chauffeur stays with you for the block, which is the point of booking that way. On separate point-to-point trips we allocate whoever is free and properly rested, and you get their name and mobile number before each pick-up.

Can my company open an account?

Yes. Approved companies are invoiced monthly with a GST tax invoice for every trip, so expense claims and cost centres stay tidy. Travel agents and DMCs book through the partner portal instead and earn commission on completed trips.
