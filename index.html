Frequently asked questions

Is the price per person or per vehicle?

Per vehicle. The fare covers everyone in the car — a sedan is one price for 1–3 passengers, and a 15-seat Sprinter is one fixed price for the whole group.

Do prices surge at busy times?

Never. Fares are fixed by suburb zone and vehicle class, day or night — the price quoted is the price you pay, with no surge and no after-hours fee.

Can I cancel or change my booking?

Changes and cancellation are free up to 24 hours before pick-up with a full refund. Inside 24 hours, call +61 481 437 772 and we'll do our best.

What if my flight is delayed?

We track your flight number and move the pick-up automatically. Airport pick-ups include meet & greet with a name board and 30 minutes' free waiting after landing — 60 minutes for international arrivals at Brisbane Airport.

How far in advance should I book?

Online bookings close 6 hours before pick-up, so we can be certain a vehicle and chauffeur are actually free for you. We recommend 24–48 hours ahead to guarantee your preferred vehicle — especially Sprinters and school-holiday dates. Inside 6 hours, call +61 481 437 772 — we still take same-day transfers whenever a vehicle is free, we just want to confirm it with you rather than have a booking form promise it.

How long is the drive from the Gold Coast to Brisbane Airport?

Allow 60–90 minutes from most Gold Coast suburbs via the M1 and Gateway Motorway, and up to 2 hours in weekday peak. When we confirm your booking we recommend a pick-up time with a safe buffer for check-in — and tolls are already in the fare.

How will I find my driver at the airport?

Your chauffeur meets you inside arrivals holding a name board, and you receive their name and mobile number before pick-up. Anything changes on the day, our 24/7 line connects you straight to dispatch.

How do I pay?

Securely online by card — Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Apple Pay and Google Pay — processed by Stripe, so card details never touch our website. Approved corporate accounts, travel agents and DMCs can be invoiced monthly.

Do I get a tax invoice?

Yes — a GST tax invoice is emailed automatically with every confirmation and receipt, ready for expense claims and business travel reporting.

Do you provide baby and child seats?

Yes — capsules, convertible seats and boosters are $15 each, added during booking and professionally fitted, so young children always travel safe and legal.

How much luggage can you take?

Sedans take 2 large bags, SUVs 4, minivans 6, and Sprinters 11–16. For oversize luggage, golf bags or prams, add the luggage trailer for $30.

How big a group can you carry?

Up to 15 passengers in one vehicle with the Mercedes Sprinter 15-Seater — one booking, one fixed price for the whole group. Larger groups run as multiple vehicles timed together.

Do you operate overnight and for early-morning flights?

24 hours, 7 days, at the same fixed price any time of day or night. Early departures are our specialty — we recommend a pick-up time based on your flight and check-in cut-off.

Do you transfer to the Brisbane International Cruise Terminal?

Yes. The cruise terminal at Pinkenba is minutes from Brisbane Airport, and cruise transfers are your suburb's Brisbane Airport fare plus a flat $25 — select ‘Brisbane Cruise Terminal’ in the booking form for an instant price. We meet disembarking passengers with a name board and track ship arrivals.

Can you take pets, wheelchairs or extra equipment?

Assistance animals are always welcome at no charge. Pets travel by prior arrangement in a suitable carrier. Foldable wheelchairs, walkers, prams, golf bags and surfboards are carried free where they fit — or add the trailer for bulky loads.

Do you do theme park transfers and private tours?

Yes — fixed-price transfers to Movie World, Dreamworld, Sea World and Wet'n'Wild, plus private day tours to Byron Bay, Springbrook and Tamborine Mountain, and hourly charter for weddings, corporate events and cruise days.

Which areas do you cover?

Every Gold Coast suburb from Coolangatta to Ormeau and the hinterland, and all Brisbane metro suburbs including the CBD, Redlands, Logan and Ipswich — to and from both airports. Tweed Heads, Kingscliff, Byron Bay, the Sunshine Coast, Toowoomba, Caboolture and Bribie Island are all published fares too — see the full list on our prices page.
