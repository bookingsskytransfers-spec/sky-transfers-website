The run home is the whole reason to book this

Getting to an event is easy. Twenty people can drive themselves, park across three car parks and meet at the gate. The problem is four hours later, when half of them have had a drink and the car park is gridlocked and somebody is quietly working out whether they are under.

An event charter removes that decision entirely. The vehicle that dropped you off is the vehicle that takes you home, the chauffeur has been waiting, and nobody is doing mental arithmetic about standard drinks in a stadium car park at eleven at night.

How event pricing works, and how it differs from our airport fares

Everywhere else on this site we publish a fixed fare by suburb. An event is different, and it is worth being straight about why.

Events are charged as an hourly charter: the vehicle and chauffeur stay with your group for a block of hours rather than being paid per trip. That is what makes the wait and the return leg possible in the first place. Driver, fuel, tolls and GST are all inside the quoted figure.

Questions

Why is an event transfer priced differently to an airport transfer?

Event transport is an hourly charter rather than a published fare. The vehicle and chauffeur stay with your group for a block of hours, so you are not paying per trip and the return leg is already covered. A half day is up to four hours and a full day up to eight, with driver, fuel, tolls and GST included.

Is there a late-night charge for the run home?

Yes. Charter pick-ups between 11pm and 5am carry a $50 after-hours charge. This is the one part of our pricing that does carry a loading — published airport fares never do, at any hour. We would rather tell you now than put it on the invoice.

How many people can travel in one vehicle?

Up to 14 in a Sprinter, 18 in the mini coach and 53 in the luxury coach. Larger groups run as multiple vehicles timed to arrive together, which is how we handle corporate boxes, sporting clubs and whole wedding parties heading on to a concert.

Can we drink on board on the way to the event?

Water and soft drinks are fine. For alcohol, Queensland rules apply and it depends on the vehicle and the occasion, so ask when you book and we will confirm what is possible for your charter rather than guess.

What happens if the event finishes later than scheduled?

The chauffeur waits. That is the point of chartering rather than booking two separate trips — encores run long, sport goes to extra time, and a vehicle already paid for through that block is not going to leave without you.

Can you collect interstate guests from the airport as well?

Yes, and those legs are published fares rather than charter rates. Flying in for an event means the airport run is priced by suburb before you book — the Gold Coast Convention Centre at Broadbeach is $110 from Gold Coast Airport, and Brisbane Entertainment Centre at Boondall is $85 from Brisbane Airport.
