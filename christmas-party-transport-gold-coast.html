Questions

How far ahead should we book Christmas party transport?

As early as you can. Friday and Saturday nights in late November and December go first, because there are only so many Sprinters and one coach.

Can you pick up from several places?

Yes. Tell us every pick-up point when you ask for a quote and we will build the route into the fixed price.

Is the price per person?

No. It is per vehicle, with GST and tolls included, so it does not climb every time somebody else says yes.

Can we pay on invoice?

Yes. The company receives one invoice with a GST tax invoice for the books, and approved accounts are billed monthly.

What if the party runs late?

Late finishes are fine. There is no after-hours or late-night surcharge, and the price we quote covers the night you describe to us.

What is the biggest vehicle?

A King Long 53-seat coach. Groups over 53 travel in several vehicles timed together.
