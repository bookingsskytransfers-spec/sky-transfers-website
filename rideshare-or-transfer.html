Rideshare, or a fixed-price transfer?

We run the transfers, so treat this as interested rather than neutral. We have still written the part where rideshare is the better answer, because pretending otherwise would waste your time and ours.

See our published fares

Start with when you should not book us

A rideshare is usually the sensible choice for a short, ordinary trip in the middle of an ordinary day. Gold Coast Airport to Kirra or Bilinga is a few minutes up the road. You are travelling alone or as a couple, you have a bag each, nothing about the day is tight, and you do not much care which car turns up. Open the app.

The same goes for the unplanned trip. Rideshare is genuinely excellent at the thing it was built for: getting a car to a person who decided thirty seconds ago that they wanted one. We close online bookings six hours ahead precisely because we are not built for that, and we would rather say so than take a booking we cannot staff properly.

So the honest framing is not which is better. It is which problem you actually have.

The real difference is when the price gets decided

Everything else follows from this one thing.

Rideshare prices are set at the moment you travel. Uber's own published explanation of surge pricing says that when demand is very high — it gives bad weather, rush hour and special events as examples — prices may increase; that riders can either pay more or wait to see whether rates come back down; and that because rates update in real time, surge can change quickly.

That is not a scandal. It is a deliberate mechanism, it is disclosed, and it is what keeps a car available at 2am on New Year's Day when nobody would otherwise be driving. For hailing, it works.

It fits badly against a flight, though. You booked the flight six weeks ago. You cannot move it because the multiplier is unfavourable on the morning, and you cannot wait to see whether rates come down when boarding closes in forty minutes. The one time you have least flexibility is the one time the price is least settled.

Our side of that. Every fare is published by suburb and vehicle before you book, and it does not move. No surge, no peak rate, no night or public holiday loading on an airport transfer — a 3am pick-up costs what a Tuesday lunchtime pick-up costs. The single exception anywhere in our pricing is charter work, where a pick-up between 11pm and 5am carries $50, and we put that on the page rather than the invoice.

Four situations where it stops being about the fare

1. The flight is late

We track the flight number and move the pick-up automatically, at no extra charge, with 30 minutes of free waiting after landing and 60 minutes for international arrivals at Brisbane Airport. You do not need to call anyone from the aircraft.

Hailing on arrival means joining whatever market exists at the minute you land. Rideshare apps do offer scheduled and reserve options, and they are worth using if you go that way — but a car hailed at 1am after a three-hour delay is being hailed into the thinnest part of the night.

2. There are more than three of you, with luggage

This is where the comparison usually breaks, and it breaks on geometry rather than price. Five adults with five suitcases do not fit in a sedan. Two rideshares means two fares, two drivers, two arrival times and a conversation in a car park about who has the keys.

A Sprinter 14-Seater takes up to 14 passengers and 15 bags on one booking at one price. The mini coach takes 18. Gold Coast Airport to Surfers Paradise is $125 in a sedan and $305 in the Sprinter — which, split across a group that would otherwise need three cars, is usually the cheaper answer as well as the less annoying one.

3. Somebody is under seven

Queensland requires children up to 7 years of age to travel in an Australian Standard approved restraint, appropriate for their age and size. That is not negotiable at the kerb, and it is not something a driver can produce on request.

Whichever option you choose, the restraint has to be arranged before the car arrives. We fit capsules, convertible seats and boosters for $15 each, booked with the vehicle — the detail is on the child seats page.

4. It is 4am, or it is a public holiday

Early departures are the most common thing we do, and they are priced the same as everything else. The hours when fewest drivers are working are exactly the hours when a fixed fare is worth the most — and also the hours when you least want to find out your ride is twenty minutes away.

Where you are standing when the car arrives

A small thing that turns out not to be small.

Gold Coast Airport's own transport page directs rideshare passengers to a designated pick-up zone outside the terminal. A booked transfer meets you inside the arrivals hall, with a name board, and you have the chauffeur's name and mobile number before the day.

With one bag and good weather, that difference is worth nothing at all. With a pram, two tired children, a car seat and rain, it is worth more than the gap in the fare.

Questions

Is a rideshare cheaper than a booked transfer?

Often, yes, particularly on a short run in the middle of the day when demand is ordinary. A rideshare from Gold Coast Airport to Kirra is a few minutes and a small fare, and booking a chauffeur for it would be over-engineering the problem. The gap closes as the trip gets longer, the group gets bigger or the hour gets less sociable.

What actually differs between the two?

It is the difference between a price set in advance and a price set at the moment you travel. Uber's own documentation explains that in periods of very high demand prices may increase, that riders can pay more or wait for rates to come down, and that surge changes quickly because it responds to real-time demand. That is a deliberate design and it works for hailing. It is simply a poor fit for a trip you booked six weeks ago to catch a specific aircraft.

What about child seats?

Queensland requires children up to 7 years of age to travel in an Australian Standard approved restraint appropriate for their age and size. A restraint cannot be improvised at the kerb, so whichever option you choose, it has to be arranged before the car arrives. We fit capsules, convertible seats and boosters for $15 each, booked with the vehicle.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

We track the flight number and move the pick-up automatically at no extra charge, with 30 minutes of free waiting after landing and 60 minutes for international arrivals at Brisbane Airport. Hailing on arrival means you join whatever market exists at the moment you land, which on a delayed late-night arrival is usually a thinner one.

Where do you actually meet the car?

At Gold Coast Airport the airport's own transport page directs rideshare passengers to a designated pick-up zone outside the terminal. A booked transfer meets you inside the arrivals hall with a name board. With one bag and good weather the difference is nothing. With a pram, two tired children and rain, it is the whole trip.

Which is better for a work trip?

A GST tax invoice is emailed automatically with every confirmation and receipt, showing a fixed figure that was agreed before travel. If someone else signs off your expenses, that is usually the practical difference rather than the fare itself.
