Who it's for

  • Airbnb and holiday-let hosts

Booking transfers for your clients yourself? That's our agent and partner program, with its own booking portal and monthly invoicing. The referral programme is for people who would rather point their guests at us and let them book.

How it works

  1. ApplyFill in the short form below. We'll call or email you to set up your account.
  2. Get your link and codeA link like skytransfers.com.au/?ref=yourname, and the same code for guests to type.
  3. Guests book and pay onlineAt the normal published fares, with the same chauffeurs and the same service as every booking.
  4. You get paid monthlyCommission on each completed trip, by bank transfer, with a statement you can check any time.

Commission rate agreed when your account is set up.

Your guests pay exactly what everyone else pays: the fares are fixed and published on our prices page, from airport transfers to minibus and coach charters. You can follow every booking you referred, and what you have earned, in your own affiliate sign-in.

The terms, in short

  • Your link remembers you for 30 days on the guest's device. If they click someone else's link after yours, the last link clicked counts.
  • A code the guest types into the booking form counts ahead of any link.
  • Commission is paid only on trips that are booked and paid on our website and then completed. A cancelled or refunded booking earns nothing.
  • It is worked out on the fare, excluding GST and extras such as child seats and the luggage trailer.
  • Bookings you make for yourself don't count, and agent-portal and account bookings are not part of the programme.
  • No paid search advertising on "Sky Transfers" or our brand name, and no spam.
  • Sky Transfers can close an account that is misused.
