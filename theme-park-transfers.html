Where the parks actually are

They are not in one place, which is the thing that catches most families out when they book accommodation. Three separate locations, and the fare follows whichever suburb you are going to.

  • Warner Bros. Movie World and Wet'n'Wild — Oxenford, on the Pacific Motorway about 25 minutes north of Surfers Paradise.
  • Dreamworld and WhiteWater World — Coomera, a few minutes further north again on the same motorway.
  • Sea World — Main Beach, on the Spit, and the only one of the four that is genuinely close to Surfers Paradise.

What it costs

Theme park transfers are priced on the suburb the park sits in, using the same published fares as everything else. One way, per vehicle, GST and tolls included.

Park day transfers, not just airport runs

Plenty of these bookings are not from an airport at all. They are from an apartment in Surfers Paradise or Broadbeach to a park gate at 9am and back at closing, because a family of five with a pram does not fit in one rideshare and the tram does not go to Oxenford.

Book both legs at once and the return is locked in at the same fixed price — which matters most at 5pm, when every family in the car park is trying to get a ride at the same moment and surge pricing is doing exactly what it is designed to do.

If you are doing two parks in a day, or want the vehicle to stay with you, hourly charter is usually better value than separate transfers. That is on the charters page.

Travelling with small children

Capsules, convertible seats and boosters are $15 each and are professionally fitted before your chauffeur arrives. They must be booked with the vehicle. Queensland restraint law is not negotiable and a seat that was not booked cannot be conjured up in the drop-off zone.

Prams travel free where they fit, and in a minivan or Sprinter they always do. If you are bringing a double pram plus a day's worth of bags, say so — the $30 luggage trailer exists for exactly this and is cheaper than a second vehicle.

Questions

Can you collect us at closing time?

Yes, and booking the return in advance is the point. The parks close at a known time and the pick-up is scheduled against it, so you are not competing with several thousand other people for a car at 5pm.

Is there a surcharge in school holidays?

No. The fare is set by suburb and vehicle class only — no surge, no holiday loading, no after-hours rate. Sprinters do book out in school holidays though, so book earlier rather than cheaper.

Can we do Movie World and Dreamworld on the same day?

They are about ten minutes apart on the M1, so it is a practical day. For that pattern ask about hourly charter rather than three separate transfers — it usually costs less and the vehicle waits with you.

How many can you take in one vehicle?

Up to 14 in a Sprinter, with 15 bags, on one booking and one fare. Extended families and school groups run this way regularly.
