Questions

How is a point-to-point fare worked out?

On the road distance between the two addresses, snapped into a fixed band, plus a flat amount for the vehicle class. The booking form measures the route and shows the price for every vehicle before you enter any details. It is a fixed price, not a meter, and the same at any hour.

Is there a minimum fare?

Yes — $80 in a sedan, the same minimum as our shortest airport fare. It covers short hops across the Gold Coast or Brisbane, such as Surfers Paradise to Broadbeach.

Do prices go up at night or on weekends?

No. Point-to-point fares and hourly hire are the same price at any hour, any day. There is no surge and no evening or weekend loading.

How does hourly hire work?

Choose By the hour in the booking form, give us a starting point and the number of hours, and your chauffeur and vehicle stay with you for that time — as many stops as you like. Three hours minimum, up to six; from $90 an hour in a sedan. Longer days are booked as charters.

Can I book a transfer to a restaurant and another one home later?

Yes — book it as a return and both legs are 10% cheaper than two separate one-ways. If you'd rather not fix the time you'll be leaving, book by the hour instead and the chauffeur waits.

What if one end is an airport?

Then it is an airport transfer, with the published fixed fare for that suburb — every suburb is on the prices page. The form works it out from the addresses you type; you don't need to choose.
