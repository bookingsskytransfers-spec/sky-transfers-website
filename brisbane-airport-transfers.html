Two terminals, and the one that catches people out

Brisbane Airport runs separate domestic and international terminals about two kilometres apart, connected by the Airtrain. Your booking records which one you are arriving at, so your chauffeur is waiting in the right building — not texting you from the wrong end of the airport while you stand at a carousel.

International arrivals get 60 minutes of free waiting after landing rather than the usual 30. That is deliberate: immigration, baggage and biosecurity at BNE regularly take longer than an hour on a full widebody, and an hour of buffer means you are not paying for a queue you did not choose to stand in.

Questions we get about BNE

How long from Brisbane Airport to the CBD?

Usually 20 to 30 minutes via the Gateway and the airport link, longer in weekday peak. We build the buffer into the recommended pick-up time on departures.

Is the Gateway toll extra?

No. Tolls and GST are inside the quoted fare on every route we publish. There is nothing added at the end of the trip.

Which terminal will the driver meet me at?

Whichever one your flight lands at — the booking captures it from your flight number. Your chauffeur meets you inside arrivals with a name board, and you have their name and mobile number in advance.

I have a connecting domestic flight after an international arrival.

Tell us both flight numbers when you book. Terminal transfers at BNE are short but the timing matters, and dispatch would rather know than guess.

Can you carry a big group off one flight?

Up to 15 in a single Sprinter with 16 bags. Beyond that we run multiple vehicles timed to arrive together, which is how conference and crew movements usually work. For anything larger, the coach charter page covers the 53-seater.
