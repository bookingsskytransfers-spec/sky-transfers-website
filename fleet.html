Questions

Is it your own fleet?

Yes. The vehicles on this page are ours and are driven by our own chauffeurs. For very large groups we time several vehicles together.

What is your biggest vehicle?

A King Long 53-seat coach: half day (up to 4 hours) $1,550, full day (up to 8 hours) $2,300. Groups over 53 travel in more than one vehicle, timed together.

What is the largest Sprinter?

The Mercedes Sprinter 18-Seater, which takes up to 18 passengers and 18 bags. It is quoted per job.

Are prices per person?

No. Every price is per vehicle and covers everyone on board, with GST and tolls included.

Is there an after-hours charge?

No. There is no after-hours or late-night surcharge on any transfer or charter.

What do child seats and the luggage trailer cost?

Child seats, capsules and boosters are $15 each, fitted before we arrive. The luggage trailer is $30.
