What we provide

  • Family cars. A Mercedes V-Class for the immediate family, so they travel together from home to the service.
  • Mourner transport. Mercedes Sprinters with 10, 14 and 18 seats and a 53-seat coach for guests, from a hotel, church or home to the crematorium, the cemetery and the wake.
  • Airport pick-ups for relatives flying in from interstate or overseas, at Gold Coast and Brisbane airports, with flight tracking. These are our published Gold Coast Airport and Brisbane Airport transfer fares.
  • Several vehicles timed together, so the family arrives together and guests are not left waiting.

Every vehicle is our own, driven by our own chauffeurs. You can see them on the fleet page.

How it works

  1. Call us, or send the details below: the date, the venues and how many people are travelling.
  2. We confirm the vehicles and the times, and liaise with your funeral director if you would like us to.
  3. The driver arrives early, waits through the service and the wake, and takes everyone home.

Booked by the hour, with a minimum of three hours, at the same rates as our chauffeur service and charters. Waiting is included. Every quote is fixed before the day, with tolls and GST included and no after-hours or late-night surcharge.

Questions

Do you provide a hearse?

No. We provide transport for family and mourners only. Your funeral director arranges the hearse.

Can the driver wait during the service and the wake?

Yes. Waiting is included in the hourly booking, so the vehicle and the driver stay with you through the service and the wake and take everyone home afterwards.

Can you collect relatives from the airport?

Yes, from Gold Coast Airport or Brisbane Airport. We track the flight and meet them inside the terminal.

How much notice do you need?

Ideally 48 hours, but call +61 481 437 772 at any time and we will do our best at short notice.

Can you liaise with our funeral director?

Yes. Give us their name and number and we will confirm the times and the order of vehicles with them directly.

How is it priced?

By the hour, with a minimum of three hours, at our published hourly and charter rates. Every quote is fixed before the day, with tolls and GST included and no after-hours or late-night surcharge.
