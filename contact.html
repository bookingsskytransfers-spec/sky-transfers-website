Where we operate

The Gold Coast, Brisbane, the Tweed and Byron Bay, with published fares to the Sunshine Coast and Toowoomba as well. We are a pre-booked chauffeur service: we come to you, so there is no office to visit. See all suburbs and fares, Gold Coast Airport transfers and Brisbane Airport transfers.

Which way to get in touch

  • An airport transfer. The booking form prices it on the spot and takes the booking. Online bookings close two hours before pick-up; inside that, call +61 481 437 772.
  • A group, a day out or an event. Request a group quote and we come back with a fixed price in writing.
  • Changing or cancelling a booking. See Manage my booking below.
  • Travel agents, DMCs and corporate accounts. See the agent and partner program.

Manage my booking

To change or cancel a booking, call or WhatsApp +61 481 437 772, or reply to your confirmation email at info@skytransfers.com.au. Give the name on the booking and the pick-up date, and the booking reference if you have it.

  • More than 24 hours before pick-up: changes and cancellation are free, with a full refund.
  • Within 24 hours of pick-up: please call. We will do our best to accommodate you, but a cancellation fee of up to the full fare may apply.

The full rules are in our booking terms, section 4.
