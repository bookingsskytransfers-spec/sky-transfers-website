Sky Transfers is the trading name of Australia Wide Car Services Pty Ltd (ABN 22 680 932 003), a private transfer and charter operator on the Gold Coast. We have been on the road for more than ten years and carry more than 15,000 travellers a year, to and from Gold Coast and Brisbane airports, the cruise terminal, hotels, weddings, conferences and events.

Our own cars and chauffeurs

The vehicles are ours: sedans, SUVs, people movers, a Lexus ES luxury sedan, the Mercedes V-Class, Mercedes Sprinters with 10, 14 and 18 seats, and a King Long 53-seat coach. They are driven by our own chauffeurs. We are not a marketplace that resells your trip to whichever operator answers, so the vehicle in your booking is the vehicle at your door. See the whole fleet.

How we price

Every price is fixed per vehicle and covers everyone on board, with GST and tolls included. Every suburb is on the published price list, and the booking form quotes the same figure. There is no surge, no meter, and no night or public-holiday loading on an airport transfer: 4am costs the same as midday. Book a return on this website and both legs are 10% off.

What every booking includes

  • A name board in arrivals, and your flight tracked so a delay does not cost you anything.
  • Free waiting: 30 minutes after landing, 60 for international arrivals at Brisbane Airport.
  • Child seats, capsules and boosters at $15 each, fitted before we arrive. How child seats work
  • A luggage trailer for golf bags, surfboards and prams for $30.

Where we go

The Gold Coast and Brisbane, Gold Coast Airport (OOL) and Brisbane Airport (BNE), the Brisbane cruise terminal, Tweed Heads, Byron Bay and Northern NSW, the Sunshine Coast, Logan, Ipswich, the Redlands and Toowoomba. Addresses that are not on the price list are priced by road distance in the booking form.
