Fly into Gold Coast if you can

Coolangatta is the clearest case on the whole price list. The airport is in the suburb, so the transfer is $80. The same journey from Brisbane Airport is $280, because it is a two-hour drive down the M1 and back along the coast.

If your flights are not booked, that $200 difference is worth more than most fare savings you will find comparing airlines. If they are already booked into Brisbane, the run is still a published fare rather than something you negotiate on arrival.

A short transfer is not a pointless one. People ask why book a car for a ten-minute drive. Because at 11pm with three bags and a tired child, ten minutes of certainty beats twenty minutes in a rank, and the fare is fixed either way.

The border, and the clocks

The Queensland–New South Wales border runs through Coolangatta — cross Boundary Street and you are in Tweed Heads. Nothing about that costs you anything on a transfer, and there is no fee or vehicle change.

What it does affect is the time. From early October to early April, New South Wales observes daylight saving and Queensland does not, so Tweed Heads runs an hour ahead of Coolangatta despite being a few hundred metres away. We schedule against your flight time in the airport's own clock, which is the safe way round — but if you are giving us a pick-up time from a Tweed address in summer, say which clock you mean. It is the single most common timing mix-up on this stretch of coast.

Surfboards

Snapper Rocks, Greenmount and Kirra are why a lot of people fly into this airport, and boards travel free where they fit. A shortboard will usually go in an SUV or minivan; a longboard or a multi-board bag will not. Add the $30 luggage trailer and tell us what is coming, so the right vehicle turns up rather than a car with a problem.

Questions

Is $80 really the whole fare?

For the vehicle, one way, GST included, one to three passengers in a sedan. There is no surge, no night rate and no minimum-fare top-up on short runs.

Do you cross into Tweed Heads and Kingscliff?

Yes, both are published fares. Byron Bay and its district are too — $220 from Gold Coast Airport. See Byron Bay transfers.

How early should I be picked up for a morning flight?

Closer than anywhere else on the coast, but we will still recommend a time built back from your check-in cut-off rather than from the drive. Being five minutes from the terminal does not shorten the bag drop queue.

Can you do a one-way drop at the airport from a Tweed hotel?

Yes. Tweed Heads and the NSW side are published fares in both directions, and the summer timezone difference is worth confirming when you book.
