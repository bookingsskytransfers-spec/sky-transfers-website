    New booking for a guest

    Amending — saving re-prices it, switches off the old payment link, makes a new one and emails the guest the updated confirmation.

    Priced exactly as the website prices it — airport fares from the table, any other two suburbs on road distance. For a job with no online price (the 18-seater, a charter, a special rate) type the agreed total in the override box. The guest gets the same branded confirmation and PDF the website sends.

    3 to 6 hours is priced at the website's hourly rate unless you type an agreed total. Anything longer (or shorter) is a charter: type the agreed total in the override box. Over 6 hours the guest's confirmation and payment link say Charter — N hours as directed. Put the itinerary in Notes.

    Stops are on the outbound journey and priced by the extra road distance they add — the price includes them automatically. Type a full street address for accuracy.

    Vehicle photo gallery

    These appear on skytransfers.com.au/gallery.html. Pictures are shrunk and re-saved here in your browser before they are sent, which also removes the location stamp a phone puts inside a photo.

    Check the number plate first. Anything uploaded here goes on the website exactly as it is — pick a photo where the plate is not readable, or one already marked SKY TRANSFERS.

    Old enquiry records

    Our privacy policy says we delete enquiries we never carried after two years. This removes website enquiries still marked requested or declined that are older than that. Paid and confirmed bookings are never touched — those are business records and are kept seven years.
