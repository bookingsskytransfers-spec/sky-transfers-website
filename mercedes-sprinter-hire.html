What people hire a Sprinter for

Airport groups. A touring party, a team or two families landing together. One vehicle meets the flight, takes everyone and the bags, and costs less per head than splitting across cars: Gold Coast Airport to Broadbeach is $200 in the Sprinter 10.

Weddings. The Sprinter 14 is the one we send most often: it moves the bridal party, then shuttles guests between the venue and the accommodation so nobody drives. More on the wedding transfers page.

Winery days and tours. Tamborine Mountain, the hinterland and the Scenic Rim by the half or full day, with the driver staying with the group. No designated driver needed.

Conferences and corporate groups. Delegates between the airport, the hotel and the venue, with a tax invoice for every booking. The Sprinter 18 takes a full table of eighteen in one run. See corporate transfers.

Theme parks, sport and nights out. Movie World, Dreamworld, a golf day or a race meeting. The fare is identical at 2am and at midday, so a late finish costs no more than an early one.

Questions

How much is Mercedes Sprinter hire with a driver?

An airport run is a fixed fare by suburb, from $165 in the Sprinter 10, $240 in the Sprinter 14 and $290 in the Sprinter 18. By the hour it is $135 an hour in the Sprinter 10, $150 in the Sprinter 14 and $165 in the Sprinter 18, for three to six hours. A half day of up to four hours is $540 in the Sprinter 10 or $590 in the Sprinter 14, and a full day of up to eight hours is $980 or $1,070; the Sprinter 18 is quoted for the day. Each figure is per vehicle with the driver and GST included.

Can I hire a Sprinter and drive it myself?

No. Every Sprinter we run comes with a professional chauffeur. If you want to drive it yourself you need a rental company, not us, and we would rather tell you now.

How many seats does a Mercedes Sprinter have?

Ours seat 10, 14 or 18 passengers. There is no nine-seat Sprinter in the fleet: for up to seven people the People Mover is the smaller and cheaper choice, and for more than eighteen we run a 53-seat coach or two vehicles together.

Will our luggage fit?

The Sprinter 10 carries 11 bags, the Sprinter 14 carries 15 and the Sprinter 18 carries 18. For golf bags, surfboards or a touring group with two cases each, add the luggage trailer for $30.

Is there a late-night or weekend charge?

No after-hours or late-night surcharge. The fare for a route is the same at 4am as at midday, and the same on a Saturday night as on a Tuesday morning.

How far ahead should I book?

Online bookings close two hours before pick-up. Weddings, conferences and school-holiday dates are best booked two to four weeks ahead, because we only have so many large vehicles. Inside two hours, call +61 481 437 772 and we will take it if a Sprinter is free.
